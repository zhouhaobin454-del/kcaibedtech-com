Smart Sleep Industry Trends: What the 138th Canton Fair 2025 Revealed

By Kang Cai Smart Sleep · Published October 25, 2025

Quick Summary

Six smart sleep trends from the 138th Canton Fair 2025 — app connectivity, Split King, zero-gravity, OEM automation, recliners, and SE Asia growth.

The 138th Canton Fair (October 15–19, 2025, Guangzhou) offered a clear signal of where the global smart sleep industry is heading. Kang Cai's team at booth 4.2A08 held direct conversations with buyers from over 30 countries across five days. Here are the six most significant trends that emerged.

1. App Connectivity Is Now a Mid-Tier Expectation

Until recently, Bluetooth and Wi-Fi app control was a premium feature reserved for high-end adjustable beds. At the 138th Canton Fair, buyers from mid-market retailers in Germany, the Netherlands, and South Korea specifically requested app connectivity as a standard feature — not an upgrade. Kang Cai's KR002 and KR003 models, both with app control, were the most inquired-about products from this buyer segment.

2. Split-Zone Demand Is Growing Fast

Split King and Split Queen configurations — where each side adjusts independently — are rapidly moving from a niche request to a mainstream requirement in North American and Scandinavian markets. Several buyers cited "his and hers" adjustment as the top consumer purchase driver in their markets. Kang Cai's KR004 supports Split King configuration natively.

3. Zero-Gravity Preset Is the New Must-Have

The zero-gravity sleep position — head raised, legs elevated, spine decompressed — has crossed from the medical recovery market into mainstream consumer awareness. At the Canton Fair, "does it have zero gravity?" was one of the most frequently asked questions from buyers across all markets. Kang Cai's KR003 and KR004 include one-touch zero-gravity presets.

4. OEM Automation Is a Competitive Differentiator

Kang Cai's booth exterior banner — "打造全球电动床行业OEM自动化制造基地" (Building the world's electric bed OEM automation manufacturing base) — resonated strongly with buyers who have faced quality inconsistency and delivery delays from non-automated suppliers. Buyers from Australia and the UK specifically cited factory automation as a key supplier selection criterion for 2026 procurement.

5. Leisure Chair Crossover Is Emerging

Electric recliner chairs that combine zero-gravity positioning, massage, and app control attracted significant interest — particularly from buyers in senior living and wellness sectors. Kang Cai's YJ001-3 and YJ002-1 captured this crossover interest, with several buyers requesting combined smart bed and leisure chair OEM programs.

6. Southeast Asian Demand Is Accelerating

Buyers from Thailand, Malaysia, Vietnam, and Indonesia were more active at smart sleep exhibits at this Canton Fair than in previous years, reflecting growing middle-class demand for premium sleep products across ASEAN markets. Kang Cai's KD001-A3 and KD010-A2 were highlighted for their adaptability to these markets' requirements — particularly space efficiency and lightweight construction for warmer-climate shipping logistics.

Conclusion

The 138th Canton Fair confirmed that the global smart sleep market is expanding across more price tiers and more geographies than at any point in the past decade. Kang Cai's product development roadmap, OEM manufacturing infrastructure, and direct buyer relationships position the company well for 2026 growth programs.
