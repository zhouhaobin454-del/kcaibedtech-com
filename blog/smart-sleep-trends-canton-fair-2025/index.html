Smart Sleep Industry Trends: What the 138th Canton Fair 2025 Revealed

By Kang Cai Smart Sleep · Published October 25, 2025 · Updated October 29, 2026

Key takeaways
  • Six trends to watch: app connectivity, split-zone demand, the zero-gravity preset, OEM automation, leisure-chair crossover and Southeast Asian demand.
  • App control is moving from a premium feature to a mid-tier expectation, and zero-gravity is now a routine question in buyer conversations.
  • The practical step for buyers: confirm functions, motor, control options and market requirements with the supplier before quotation.

The 138th Canton Fair (October 15–19, 2025, Guangzhou) offered a clear signal of where the global smart sleep industry is heading. Kang Cai exhibited at booth 4.2A08. Here are six smart sleep trends worth watching, drawn from the fair and the wider market.

1. App Connectivity Is Now a Mid-Tier Expectation

Until recently, Bluetooth and Wi-Fi app control was a premium feature reserved for high-end adjustable beds. Mid-market programs increasingly ask for app connectivity as a standard feature rather than an upgrade.

2. Split-Zone Demand Is Growing Fast

Split King and Split Queen configurations — where each side adjusts independently — are rapidly moving from a niche request to a mainstream requirement in North American and Scandinavian markets. Independent "his and hers" adjustment is a common reason couples choose a split format.

3. Zero-Gravity Preset Is the New Must-Have

The zero-gravity sleep position — head raised, legs elevated, spine decompressed — has crossed from the medical recovery market into mainstream consumer awareness. "Does it have zero gravity?" is now a routine question in buyer conversations across markets. Kang Cai's KD004-B1 electric mattress lists a zero-gravity mode among its preset modes.

4. OEM Automation Is a Competitive Differentiator

Kang Cai's booth exterior banner — "打造全球电动床行业OEM自动化制造基地" (Building the world's electric bed OEM automation manufacturing base) — reflects the company's focus on automated, repeatable OEM production.

5. Leisure Chair Crossover Is Emerging

Electric recliner chairs that combine zero-gravity positioning, massage, and app control attracted significant interest — particularly from buyers in senior living and wellness sectors. Kang Cai's YJ yoga chairs, with a V-shaped lift and one-touch yoga mode, speak to this crossover, and some buyers asked about combined smart bed and chair programs.

6. Southeast Asian Demand Is Accelerating

Demand for premium sleep products is growing across ASEAN markets, reflecting a growing middle class in Thailand, Malaysia, Vietnam and Indonesia.

Conclusion

The smart sleep market is widening across price tiers and regions. For buyers, the practical step is to confirm the functions, motor, control options and market requirements of each product with the supplier before quotation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Split King and Split Queen configurations let each side adjust independently, a common reason couples choose a split format. They are moving from a niche request toward a mainstream one in North American and Scandinavian markets.
