Split King or King? Choosing the Right Adjustable Frame Format

By Kang Cai Smart Sleep · Published November 7, 2025 · Updated October 29, 2026

Key takeaways
  • A King base is one platform that adjusts as a single unit; a Split King is two Twin XL bases side by side that adjust independently.
  • Split King needs two motor systems and typically two Twin XL mattresses, and takes more shipping volume per bed.
  • Confirm whether controls are independent (two remotes), synchronized (one app controlling both sides) or a hybrid.

Split King and King are two different adjustable bed configurations that serve different market segments and consumer needs. Understanding the difference is essential for B2B buyers specifying adjustable base programs — particularly for North American and Australian markets where King configurations are common.

What Is a King Adjustable Base?

A King adjustable base is a single platform that spans the full King mattress width (typically 193 cm / 76 inches). The entire base adjusts as one unit: when the head section rises, both sides rise together. This is the standard configuration for most entry-to-mid-range adjustable bed programs. It is simpler to manufacture, easier to transport, and lower in cost than a Split King.

What Is a Split King Adjustable Base?

A Split King consists of two Twin XL (single long) adjustable bases placed side by side to fill a King-size space. Each side adjusts completely independently — one partner can raise the head section to read while the other lies flat. This configuration requires two separate motor systems, two separate remotes (or a coordinated app interface), and typically two separate mattresses (Twin XL).

Who Buys Split King?

Split King programs are targeted at the premium residential market, typically:

  • Couples where sleeping preferences diverge significantly
  • Consumers with specific health or comfort requirements for one partner
  • Premium sleep retailers and direct-to-consumer brands positioning at the luxury segment

In North America and Scandinavia, Split King demand is growing rapidly. In most other markets (Europe outside Scandinavia, Asia Pacific, Middle East), King as a single unit remains the dominant specification.

Procurement Considerations

  • MOQ: Split King programs may require higher minimum orders due to the two-base configuration and coordination of control systems.
  • Mattress compatibility: Split King bases require two Twin XL mattresses. If your program includes mattresses, confirm sizing with your mattress supplier.
  • Packaging: Two separate bases require more shipping volume per bed unit. Factor this into container loading calculations.
  • Control system: Confirm whether controls are independent (two remotes), synchronized (one app controlling both sides), or a hybrid.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a King and a Split King adjustable base?

A King base is a single platform that adjusts as one unit. A Split King is two Twin XL adjustable bases side by side, each adjusting completely independently.

Who buys Split King?

Mostly the premium residential market: couples whose sleeping preferences diverge, consumers with specific comfort requirements for one partner, and premium sleep or direct-to-consumer brands.

What should I consider when procuring Split King?

Minimum order quantities, whether you need two Twin XL mattresses, the extra shipping volume per bed, and whether controls are independent, synchronized or hybrid.
