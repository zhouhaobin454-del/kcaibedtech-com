KD Series Electric Adjustable Bed Frames at Canton Fair 2025

By Kang Cai Smart Sleep · Published October 22, 2025

Quick Summary

Nine KD-series electric bed frames live at Canton Fair 2025, booth 4.2A08 — from entry KD001-A3 to foldable KD011-A3. OEM from 20 pcs.

The KD series is Kang Cai's core product line — electric adjustable bed frames built for global OEM and ODM programs. At the 138th Canton Fair (booth 4.2A08), nine KD-series frames were operational on the booth floor, with buyers able to test every lift function, remote, and positioning preset in person.

Models on Display

  • KD001-A3: Entry-level standard frame; backrest 0–60°, footrest 0–40°, 15/18 mm eco plywood slats
  • KD001-A6: Modular slatted frame with separate platform and lifting mechanism
  • KD002-B1: Mid-range with enhanced motor and slat system
  • KD003-B1: Three-zone frame for targeted lumbar support
  • KD004-B1: Four-zone frame with optional OKIN motor
  • KD005-A1: Five-zone independent adjustment system
  • KD008-A1: Multifunctional frame with broad motor compatibility
  • KD010-A2: Panel-choice frame: eco board or honeycomb aluminum
  • KD011-A3: Compact foldable design for space-efficient shipping

Live Demonstrations at the Booth

Each frame was demonstrated continuously throughout the fair. Staff operated the remotes and invited buyers to test the adjustment range firsthand. The KD011-A3's folding mechanism attracted particular attention — its compact flat-pack shipping format reduces container utilization by up to 40% compared to conventional frames, a major cost benefit highlighted in buyer meetings.

The KD004-B1 and KD010-A2 were the most discussed models in sourcing conversations, driven by demand from European buyers who needed aluminum panel construction for lighter shipments.

OEM Technical Specifications

  • Backrest lift: 0–60° | Footrest lift: 0–40°
  • Structure: high-strength steel frame with strong impact resistance
  • Slat options: 15/18 mm eco-friendly multi-layer plywood or honeycomb aluminum panel
  • Motor: Kang Cai standard or optional German OKIN
  • Endurance: 10,000+ cycle tested | Standard size: 2000 × 900 mm; custom available

All KD-series frames support full OEM and ODM customization including private labeling, packaging design, and motor specification. Minimum order quantities start from 20 units with approximately 45-day lead time from order confirmation.
