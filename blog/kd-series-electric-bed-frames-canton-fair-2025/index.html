KD Series Electric Adjustable Bed Frames at Canton Fair 2025

By Kang Cai Smart Sleep · Published October 22, 2025 · Updated October 29, 2026

Key takeaways
  • The KD series is Kang Cai’s line of electric adjustable bed frames and electric mattresses for OEM and ODM programs.
  • KD010-A2 lists backrest 0–60°, leg rest 0–40°, 15/18 mm eco plywood or honeycomb aluminum panel options, and a 10,000+ cycle test.
  • Motors are optional German OKIN or Haojiang; other sizes are available on request.

The KD series is Kang Cai's core product line — electric adjustable bed frames and electric mattresses built for global OEM and ODM programs. At the 138th Canton Fair (booth 4.2A08), KD-series products were shown on the booth floor, with buyers able to try the lift functions and remotes in person.

Models on Display

  • KD001-A3: Fixed electric frame; surface padding of 1 cm blue super sponge and 1 cm hard white sponge
  • KD001-A6: Fixed electric frame with a metal / aluminum-plate bed surface
  • KD005-A1: Five-zone electric frame, 2000 × 900 mm
  • KD008-A1: Multifunctional electric frame, 2000 × 900 mm
  • KD010-A2: Disassembled frame with a panel choice: eco board or honeycomb aluminum
  • KD011-A3: Disassembled frame with a surround frame, for US-style sizes

Live Demonstrations at the Booth

The frames were demonstrated at the booth, with staff operating the remotes and inviting buyers to try the adjustment range. The KD-series electric mattresses (such as KD004-B1, KD002-B1 and KD003-B1) are covered on their own product pages.

OEM Technical Specifications

  • KD010-A2 adjustment: backrest 0–60° | leg rest 0–40°
  • Structure: high-quality steel for impact resistance
  • Slat options: 15/18 mm eco-friendly multi-layer plywood or honeycomb aluminum panel
  • Motor: optional German OKIN or Haojiang
  • Endurance: 10,000+ cycle tested (KD010-A2) | Size: 2000 × 900 mm shown; other sizes on request

All KD-series frames support full OEM and ODM customization including private labeling, packaging design, and motor specification. Minimum order quantities start from 20 units with approximately 45-day lead time from order confirmation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which KD frames were shown at the 2025 Canton Fair?

KD001-A3, KD001-A6, KD005-A1, KD008-A1, KD010-A2 and KD011-A3.

What adjustment range does the KD010-A2 offer?

Backrest 0–60° and leg rest 0–40°, with a 10,000+ cycle endurance test listed.

What motor and slat options do KD frames have?

The motor is optional German OKIN or Haojiang. KD010-A2 offers 15/18 mm eco-friendly multi-layer plywood or a honeycomb aluminum panel.
