Approving an Adjustable Frame Sample: A Checklist Before Production

By Kang Cai Smart Sleep · Published November 16, 2025 · Updated October 29, 2026

Key takeaways
  • Inspect six areas of the sample: frame and structure, motor and actuator, control system, slat system, branding and packaging, and compliance documents.
  • Run head and foot lift through the full range 10+ times and test that the anti-collision stop works.
  • Confirm the motor brand matches what was agreed and request the test reports for your destination market.

Approving an OEM sample is one of the most critical steps in an adjustable bed sourcing program. A thorough sample validation process catches issues before production — when fixing them is still fast and low-cost. This checklist covers what to inspect when you receive an adjustable bed frame sample from a Chinese OEM manufacturer.

Frame and Structure

  • Check weld quality at all frame joints — look for clean, consistent welds without spatter, pitting, or incomplete fusion
  • Inspect powder coating coverage and finish — no bare metal, chips, or uneven texture
  • Verify frame dimensions against agreed specification (width, length, leg height)
  • Check frame rigidity — no flexing or racking when weight is applied
  • Inspect leg adjusters and leveling feet for correct fitment and function

Motor and Actuator

  • Run head and foot lift through full adjustment range 10+ times — check for smooth, consistent movement
  • Listen for motor noise — confirm it meets your acceptable noise level specification
  • Verify motor speed meets agreed specification
  • Test anti-collision safety — press the frame against an obstruction during adjustment and confirm it stops
  • Check motor housing fitment and mounting security
  • Confirm motor brand matches what was agreed (especially if OKIN was specified)

Control System

  • Test all remote or app functions — each button/function individually
  • Verify presets function correctly (zero-gravity, flat, custom memory positions)
  • Check wireless range — test at 5–8 m from the base
  • Confirm USB port function if specified
  • Test Bluetooth pairing and app function if specified

Slat System

  • Count slats and verify against specification
  • Check slat flexibility — flex each slat by hand to confirm no cracks or brittleness
  • Verify slat connectors are correctly seated and secure
  • Confirm slats cover the full width of the frame without gaps beyond specification

Branding and Packaging

  • Verify brand labels, logos, and model numbers match approved artwork
  • Confirm label placement matches agreed positions
  • Inspect carton construction — corner protection, internal foam padding, carton weight rating
  • Check assembly instructions for accuracy and print quality
  • Verify all hardware (bolts, tools) is included in the package

Compliance and Documentation

  • Request applicable test reports for your destination market
  • Confirm plug and voltage configuration matches your market specification
  • Check warning labels are present and in the correct language(s)

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I test on the motor and actuator?

Run head and foot lift through the full range 10+ times for smooth, consistent movement, listen for noise, check the speed against the agreement, test the anti-collision stop, and confirm the motor brand (especially if OKIN was specified).

How do I check the control system on a sample?

Test each remote or app function individually, verify the presets (zero-gravity, flat, memory), check wireless range at 5–8 m, and confirm USB and Bluetooth pairing if specified.

Which documents should I request with the sample?

The applicable test reports for your destination market, confirmation of plug and voltage, and warning labels in the correct language(s).
