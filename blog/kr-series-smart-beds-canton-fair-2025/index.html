KR001 to KR004: Smart Bed Series at Canton Fair 2025

By Kang Cai Smart Sleep · Published October 21, 2025

Quick Summary

KR001–KR004 smart beds live at Canton Fair 2025 booth 4.2A08 — app control, zero-gravity, massage. OEM/ODM programs available from Kang Cai.

The KR smart bed series is the flagship product line from Kang Cai, combining a fully upholstered premium bed platform with an integrated electric adjustable base. At booth 4.2A08 of the 138th Canton Fair, all four KR models were on live display — each operating with wireless remote and demonstrating a range of powered adjustment features.

The KR Series Lineup

Kang Cai's smart bed collection spans four models, stepping up in capability and feature set:

  • KR001: Entry-level smart bed with head and foot lift, wireless remote, eco-friendly upholstery
  • KR002: Mid-range with USB charging ports, under-bed ambient lighting, and Bluetooth app connectivity
  • KR003: Premium tier with zero-gravity preset, app control, voice-command compatibility, and whisper-quiet motor
  • KR004: Top-of-range with dual-zone massage, anti-snore auto lift, Split King configuration, and smart home integration

What Canton Fair Buyers Noticed

The KR series attracted the highest foot traffic of any product category in the Kang Cai booth. Buyers from European retail chains and North American sleep product importers spent the most time with the KR003 and KR004 models, testing the zero-gravity preset and Split King adjustment independence. The integrated headboard-and-base design — which eliminates the need for a separate bed frame — was cited as a key advantage for retailers targeting the premium residential market.

OEM and ODM Customization

All KR models are available for OEM and ODM programs. Customization options include upholstery material (fabric, PU leather, genuine leather, velvet), custom color matching to buyer branding, size (Twin through Split King; custom dimensions on ODM basis), motor choice (standard or German OKIN), and full control system configuration (remote, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi smart home). Full white-label packaging and product labeling are supported from order stage.

Technical Specifications

Backrest adjustment range: 0–60°. Footrest adjustment range: 0–40°. Lifting system endurance-tested to over 10,000 cycles. Whisper-quiet motor design. Compliant with international electrical safety standards. Available in standard and Split configurations for all KR models.
