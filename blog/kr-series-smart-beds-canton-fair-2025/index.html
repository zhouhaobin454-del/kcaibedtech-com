KR001 to KR004: Smart Bed Series at Canton Fair 2025

By Kang Cai Smart Sleep · Published October 21, 2025 · Updated October 29, 2026

Key takeaways
  • The KR series pairs a leather-upholstered frame with an integrated electric mattress from the KD range; there are four models, KR001 to KR004.
  • KR003 uses the KD002-B1 motion mattress with left / right independent backrest lift; KR004 uses the KD003-B1 with natural latex and two motors.
  • Adjustment data from the paired mattresses: backrest 0–55° and leg rest 0–35°. Motors are optional German OKIN or Haojiang.

The KR series pairs a leather-upholstered bed frame with an integrated electric mattress. At booth 4.2A08 of the 138th Canton Fair, the KR range was on display, showing how frame and mattress are presented together as one complete bed.

The KR Series Lineup

Kang Cai's smart bed collection has four models. Each is a leather-upholstered frame paired with an electric mattress from the KD range:

  • KR001: Frame 2000 × 1800 mm, shown with the KD001-B1 mattress (2000 × 1800 × 500 mm)
  • KR002: Frame 2000 × 1800 mm, shown with the KD001-B2 mattress (2000 × 1800 × 530 mm, breathable 3D material, pocket springs)
  • KR003: Frame 2000 × 1800 mm, shown with the KD002-B1 motion mattress, which has left / right independent backrest lift
  • KR004: Frame 2000 × 1500 mm, shown with the KD003-B1 mattress, which has natural latex and two motors

What Canton Fair Buyers Noticed

The integrated headboard-and-mattress presentation shows buyers a complete, finished bed rather than a separate frame and mattress. For furniture and mattress brands, that makes a KR bed easy to evaluate as a single product. Detailed specifications for each model are on its product page.

OEM and ODM Customization

KR models are available for OEM and ODM programs. Upholstery, mattress configuration, sizes and packaging are confirmed per project; contact us with your market and quantity and we will confirm what is available for each model.

Technical Specifications

The adjustment data comes from the paired mattresses: backrest 0–55° and leg rest 0–35°, with a lifting system endurance test of 10,000+ cycles listed for the KD001-B1. Optional motors are German OKIN or Haojiang, with wired or wireless remote control, and voice and APP control are available as options. Other specifications are confirmed with the quotation.
