138th Canton Fair 2025 Recap: Booth 4.2A08, Guangzhou

By Kang Cai Smart Sleep · Published October 20, 2025 · Updated October 29, 2026

Key takeaways
  • Kang Cai exhibited at the 138th Canton Fair (October 15–19, 2025, Guangzhou), booth 4.2A08 in Hall 4.2.
  • The booth showed live models across four categories: KR smart beds, KD electric frames, KD electric mattresses and YJ yoga chairs.
  • The exhibit presented Kang Cai as an OEM and ODM manufacturer, with a consultation zone for buyers.

From October 15 to 19, 2025, Kang Cai Smart Sleep Co., Ltd. (东莞市康彩智能科技有限公司) participated in the 138th China Import and Export Fair (Canton Fair) in Guangzhou. The company occupied booth 4.2A08 in Hall 4.2, presenting its full lineup of electric adjustable beds, smart bed systems, and leisure recliner chairs to global B2B buyers.

Event Overview

The 138th Canton Fair is held in three phases and draws international buyers from many markets. Kang Cai's booth in the furniture and home products hall ran over the five-day event, with product demonstrations across the main product categories.

The booth carried the brand tagline "引领科技，智造健康睡眠" (Leading Technology, Crafting Healthy Sleep) and prominently communicated the company's OEM and ODM manufacturing capabilities. The booth exterior banner declared: "打造全球电动床行业OEM自动化制造基地" — a commitment to building a globally leading automated OEM production base for the electric bed industry.

Products on Display

Across the booth floor, buyers could interact with live models across four categories:

  • Smart Beds (KR Series): KR001, KR002, KR003, KR004 — leather-upholstered frames shown with electric mattresses
  • Electric Adjustable Bed Frames (KD Series): KD001-A3, KD001-A6, KD005-A1, KD008-A1, KD010-A2, KD011-A3
  • Electric Mattresses (KD Series): KD001-B2, KD002-B1, KD003-B1, KD004-B1
  • Yoga Chairs (YJ Series): YJ001-A01 and YJ001-A02 powered zero-gravity yoga chairs

OEM and ODM Manufacturing Focus

A central message throughout Kang Cai's Canton Fair exhibit was its position as a dedicated OEM and ODM manufacturer. Kang Cai's factory is in Shatian Town, Dongguan, Guangdong Province. At the Canton Fair, buyers met directly with engineers and product managers in a dedicated consultation zone within the booth.

Connect with Kang Cai

Buyers who visited booth 4.2A08 discussed OEM programs, custom specifications, minimum order quantities (from 20 pcs), and lead times (approximately 45 days). Kang Cai's team is available year-round for inquiries beyond the fair season.
