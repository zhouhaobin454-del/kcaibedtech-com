138th Canton Fair 2025 Recap: Booth 4.2A08, Guangzhou

By Kang Cai Smart Sleep · Published October 20, 2025

Quick Summary

Kang Cai at the 138th Canton Fair, booth 4.2A08 — electric adjustable beds, smart beds and leisure chairs for global B2B buyers, Guangzhou Oct 2025.

From October 15 to 19, 2025, Kang Cai Smart Sleep Co., Ltd. (东莞市康彩智能科技有限公司) participated in the 138th China Import and Export Fair (Canton Fair) in Guangzhou. The company occupied booth 4.2A08 in Hall 4.2, presenting its full lineup of electric adjustable beds, smart bed systems, and leisure recliner chairs to global B2B buyers.

Event Overview

The 138th Canton Fair drew tens of thousands of international buyers across its three phases. Kang Cai's booth in the furniture and home products hall attracted buyers from Europe, North America, Southeast Asia, the Middle East, and South America over the five-day event. The blue-themed 200 m² exhibit space featured live-operating product demonstrations across all major product categories.

The booth carried the brand tagline "引领科技，智造健康睡眠" (Leading Technology, Crafting Healthy Sleep) and prominently communicated the company's OEM and ODM manufacturing capabilities. The booth exterior banner declared: "打造全球电动床行业OEM自动化制造基地" — a commitment to building a globally leading automated OEM production base for the electric bed industry.

Products on Display

Across the booth floor, buyers could interact with 15+ live models across four categories:

  • Smart Beds (KR Series): KR001, KR002, KR003, KR004 — fully operational with wireless remote and app control demonstrations
  • Electric Adjustable Bed Frames (KD Series): KD001-A3, KD001-A6, KD002-B1, KD003-B1, KD004-B1, KD005-A1, KD008-A1, KD010-A2, KD011-A3 — all adjustable live at the booth
  • Adjustable Mattresses: KD001-B2, KD002-B1, KD004-B1 compatible mattresses
  • Leisure Chairs (YJ Series): YJ001-3, YJ002-1 — electric recliners available for seating demos

OEM and ODM Manufacturing Focus

A central message throughout Kang Cai's Canton Fair exhibit was its position as a dedicated OEM and ODM manufacturer. The company's 25,000 m² factory in Shatian Town, Dongguan, Guangdong Province has manufactured electric adjustable bed frames since 2004. At the Canton Fair, buyers met directly with engineers and product managers in a dedicated consultation zone within the booth.

Connect with Kang Cai

Buyers who visited booth 4.2A08 discussed OEM programs, custom specifications, minimum order quantities (from 20 pcs), and lead times (approximately 45 days). Kang Cai's team is available year-round for inquiries beyond the fair season.
