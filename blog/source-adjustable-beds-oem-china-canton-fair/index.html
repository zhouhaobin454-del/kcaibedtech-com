How to Source OEM Adjustable Beds from China: A Canton Fair 2025 Guide

By Kang Cai Smart Sleep · Published October 24, 2025

Quick Summary

How to source OEM electric adjustable beds at the Canton Fair — factory credentials, MOQ, motor options and what Kang Cai offers B2B buyers.

The Canton Fair is the world's largest trade fair and one of the most efficient environments for global buyers to identify, evaluate, and begin sourcing relationships with Chinese manufacturers. For buyers in the electric adjustable bed and smart sleep category, the 138th Canton Fair provided a concentrated opportunity to compare OEM manufacturers side by side in a single exhibition hall.

1. Factory Credentials and Manufacturing Capacity

Serious sourcing conversations at the Canton Fair begin with factory verification. Kang Cai operates a 25,000 m² manufacturing facility in Shatian Town, Dongguan City, Guangdong Province — one of China's primary furniture and electronics manufacturing clusters. The factory has produced electric adjustable bed frames since 2004 and supports 100 team members including 50+ engineers.

When evaluating a Canton Fair manufacturer, ask for: factory inspection certificate, production capacity (units per month), quality management documentation, and references from existing OEM clients in your target market.

2. OEM Flexibility and MOQ

A strong OEM partner should support private labeling, packaging customization, and specification adjustments without requiring enormous minimum quantities. Kang Cai's OEM programs start at 20 units MOQ with standard lead times of approximately 45 days. ODM programs (where the buyer provides a new design for Kang Cai to manufacture) have lead times and MOQs that vary by design complexity.

3. Motor Options and Quality Standards

The motor is the most critical long-term quality factor in an adjustable bed frame. Kang Cai offers its standard in-house motor (10,000+ cycle endurance tested) and the optional German OKIN motor on select KD and KR series models. OKIN is the world's leading electric linear actuator brand, widely specified by premium bed brands in Germany, the US, and Australia.

4. Sample and Prototype Process

Reputable OEM manufacturers at the Canton Fair discuss sample timelines, prototype customization, and pre-production approval processes. Kang Cai provides buyer samples for evaluation before committing to a production order, with customization applied from the first sample stage.

5. Starting Your Inquiry

Buyers who met Kang Cai at the 138th Canton Fair are encouraged to continue conversations directly. Share your target category (adjustable frame, smart bed, leisure chair), destination market, size requirements, and estimated annual volume. Kang Cai's team will respond with a tailored product recommendation and preliminary pricing within one business day.
