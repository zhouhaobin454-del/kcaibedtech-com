YJ Series Leisure Recliner Chairs at Canton Fair 2025

By Kang Cai Smart Sleep · Published October 23, 2025 · Updated October 29, 2026

Key takeaways
  • YJ001-A01 and YJ001-A02 are powered zero-gravity yoga chairs, 700 × 1900 × 480 mm, with three motors (head, foot and leg).
  • Adjustment is backrest 0–60° and leg rest 0–45°, with a V-shaped lift and a one-touch yoga mode.
  • Optional functions are vibration massage and USB, with wireless remote and voice control; leather type and stitching are confirmed per project.

Alongside its electric bed frame and smart bed ranges, Kang Cai presented its YJ yoga chair series at the 138th Canton Fair (booth 4.2A08). The YJ yoga chairs are powered zero-gravity recliners that bring Kang Cai's motor and mechanism expertise into seating.

YJ001-A01: Yoga Chair A01

The A01 is a lie / sit dual-use recliner measuring 700 × 1900 × 480 mm, with three motors (head, foot and leg) giving backrest 0–60° and leg rest 0–45° adjustment. A V-shaped lift supports the waist and gently compresses the abdomen, and a one-touch yoga mode moves the chair into position. It is offered in technology leather or top-grain half-leather over natural latex and high-density foam, with dotted or striped stitching (catalog numbers YJ001-1 and YJ001-3).

YJ001-A02: Yoga Chair B02

The B02 style shares the same functions and the 700 × 1900 × 480 mm size, in the same two leather options (catalog numbers YJ002-4 and YJ002-6). Optional functions across both styles include vibration massage and USB, with wireless remote and voice control.

Target Markets

  • Residential furniture retailers seeking electric seating to complement smart bed programs
  • Hospitality buyers (hotels, serviced apartments, senior living) requiring durable, stylish recliner seating
  • Wellness and rehabilitation centers that need zero-gravity-capable seating
  • OEM furniture brands looking to expand into electric seating categories

Customization Options

YJ-series chairs support OEM and ODM customization. Leather type, stitching style, labeling and packaging are confirmed per project. MOQ starts from 20 pcs with a typical lead time of around 45 days, subject to model and configuration.
