YJ Series Leisure Recliner Chairs at Canton Fair 2025

By Kang Cai Smart Sleep · Published October 23, 2025

Quick Summary

YJ001-3 and YJ002-1 electric leisure recliner chairs at Canton Fair 2025 — smart relaxation seating with premium upholstery, OEM programs available.

Alongside its electric bed frame and smart bed ranges, Kang Cai presented two models from its YJ leisure chair series at the 138th Canton Fair (booth 4.2A08). The YJ001-3 and YJ002-1 are electric recliner chairs that bring Kang Cai's motor and mechanism expertise into premium seating.

YJ001-3: Fabric Leisure Recliner

The YJ001-3 is upholstered in a soft linen-blend fabric and features a smooth electric recline mechanism driven by a whisper-quiet motor. The high curved backrest, integrated pillow headrest, and armrest side pockets provide full relaxation comfort. The cross-base design creates a visually light silhouette while maintaining structural stability.

At the Canton Fair, the YJ001-3 in light grey was the most tested product among hospitality-focused buyers. Its low-profile sitting height, smooth recline feel, and upholstery quality were consistently praised in buyer feedback sessions.

YJ002-1: Premium Leather Recliner

The YJ002-1 steps up to a dark PU leather finish and a deeper recline capability. The quilted backrest, padded armrests, and independently adjustable headrest tilt give it a premium feel suitable for high-end residential and boutique hotel applications. The electric recline mechanism is operated by a discreet side panel or optional wireless handset.

Both models demonstrated full recline cycles without interruption for five days at the Canton Fair, drawing attention for their smooth mechanical consistency and stitching quality.

Target Markets

  • Residential furniture retailers seeking electric seating to complement smart bed programs
  • Hospitality buyers (hotels, serviced apartments, senior living) requiring durable, stylish recliner seating
  • Wellness and rehabilitation centers that need zero-gravity-capable seating
  • OEM furniture brands looking to expand into electric seating categories

Customization Options

YJ-series chairs support full OEM customization: upholstery (fabric, PU leather, genuine leather), color matching to brand requirements, logo and labeling, base finish (matte black, brushed gold, chrome), and packaging design. Standard sizes are available; ODM programs can accommodate custom dimensions and mechanism specifications. MOQ starts from 20 units; lead time approximately 45–60 days.
