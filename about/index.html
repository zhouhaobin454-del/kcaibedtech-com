Kang Cai is a smart sleep product manufacturer based in Qisha Industrial Park, Shatian Town, Dongguan City, Guangdong Province, China. The company manufactures adjustable bed frames, integrated smart beds, adjustable mattresses and electric leisure chairs for global B2B partners.

With 22 years of manufacturing experience in adjustable beds and actuator systems, Kang Cai supports OEM and ODM programs for furniture brands, mattress companies, distributors and hospitality operators worldwide.
