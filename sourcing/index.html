The OEM Procurement Process

A six-step framework for sourcing adjustable beds from a Chinese OEM manufacturer.

01

Research & Define Requirements

Identify your target market, product category, required functions and estimated order quantity. The more specific your specification, the more accurate the quotes you will receive.

02

Prepare Your RFQ

Write a clear RFQ covering model or functional requirements, sizes, electrical specification, branding needs and delivery terms. Use our RFQ checklist to avoid common mistakes.

03

Evaluate Manufacturers

Review factory credentials, product range depth, OEM track record, quality control processes and communication responsiveness. Request references or visit the factory if possible.

04

Request and Validate Samples

Order production-representative samples before committing to a bulk order. Inspect frame quality, motor function, control systems, slats and packaging against your specification.

05

Confirm Terms and Place Order

Agree on pricing, MOQ, lead time, payment terms, incoterms and compliance documentation. Confirm production schedule and pre-shipment inspection arrangements.

06

Production and Inspection

Monitor production milestones. Arrange a pre-shipment inspection (in-house or third-party) before the cargo is released. Confirm all branding and packaging against approved samples.

Sourcing FAQ

What is Kang Cai's standard MOQ?

Standard MOQ is from 20 pcs. MOQ varies by model and configuration. Programs using German OKIN motors typically require higher minimums. Final MOQ is confirmed at the quotation stage.

How long does OEM production take?

Typical production lead time is around 45 days, subject to model, configuration and order quantity. Confirm your required delivery date during initial inquiry so the team can flag any constraints.

Can Kang Cai develop a custom product (ODM)?

Yes. Kang Cai supports ODM programs — designing or co-designing a product to your specification. Discuss your functional requirements, target cost and development timeline with our team.

What branding can be applied?

OEM branding can be applied to product labels, the wireless remote, outer cartons and documentation. Specific options are confirmed by model and program.

What compliance documents are available?

Available test reports and compliance documentation vary by product and destination market. Contact Kang Cai to confirm which documents are available for your program and market.

Can I visit the factory?

Yes. Factory visits are welcome and encouraged for serious buyers. Kang Cai's facility is located in Shatian Town, Dongguan, Guangdong Province. Contact us to arrange a visit.
