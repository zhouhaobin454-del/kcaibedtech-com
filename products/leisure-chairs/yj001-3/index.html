YJ001-3 Yoga Chair A01 — Top-Grain Leather

The YJ001-3 is the top-grain leather / half-leather version of the A01 yoga recliner series. It shares the same electric zero-gravity design and V-shaped backrest as the YJ001-1, designed for waist support and abdominal exercise. The half-leather construction combines top-grain leather on contact surfaces with complementary material on the back. Powered by optional German OKIN motor with wireless remote. Optional voice control, vibration massage and USB port. Size: 700×1900×480 mm.

  • Backrest lift 0–60°, footrest lift 0–45°
  • V-shaped backrest for waist support and abdominal fat reduction
  • Top-grain leather / half-leather upholstery over high-quality steel frame
  • German OKIN motor optional; wireless remote (voice control & USB optional)
