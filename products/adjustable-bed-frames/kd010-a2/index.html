Product Features

Key features

01

Split lifting design

The lifting system and the mattress are separate, so the base can be paired with compatible mattresses.

02

Independent adjustment

Backrest lift 0–60° and leg rest lift 0–40°.

03

Strong structure

High-quality steel for impact resistance; lifting system endurance tested to 10,000+ cycles.

04

Panel options

Eco multi-layer board (15 / 18 mm) or a high-strength honeycomb aluminum panel.

05

Motors and control

Optional German OKIN or Haojiang motor; optional wired remote, wireless remote or Sleepace app.

06

Optional functions

Voice control, APP, vibration massage, sleep monitoring and anti-snore.
