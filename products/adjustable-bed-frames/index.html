Adjustable Bed Frames FAQ

What is the standard MOQ?

Standard MOQ is from 20 pcs. Programs using German OKIN motors may require higher minimums. Final MOQ is confirmed during quotation.

What is the typical lead time?

Typical production lead time is around 45 days, subject to model, configuration and order quantity.

Is the German OKIN motor available?

Yes, the OKIN motor is available on the KD005-A1 and KD008-A1. Programs using OKIN typically require higher MOQs.

What can be customized for OEM programs?

Options may include motor selection, plywood thickness, dimensions, fabric, packaging and private-label branding. Availability is confirmed by model.

How are compliance documents handled?

Documentation is confirmed according to the selected model, components and destination market. Contact us to discuss your requirements.

Can I estimate container loading before ordering?

Yes. Approximate loading quantities can be calculated from model, packing dimensions and container type. Final quantities are confirmed at quotation stage.
