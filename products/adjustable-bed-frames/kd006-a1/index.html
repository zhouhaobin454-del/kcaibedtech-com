Buyer Questions

KD006-A1 B2B buyer questions

What are the typical MOQ and lead time?

Standard MOQ is from 20 pcs and typical production lead time is around 45 days, subject to model, configuration and order quantity. Final terms are confirmed at the quotation stage.

Which compliance documents are available?

Available test reports and compliance documentation vary by product and destination market. Contact Kang Cai to confirm which documents are available for your program and market.

What branding can be applied?

OEM branding can be applied to product labels, the wireless remote, outer cartons and documentation. Specific options are confirmed by model and program.

What should I provide before quotation?

Your target market, required size, estimated quantity, upholstery and control preferences, plug and voltage, and the delivery date you need.

Which sizes and markets does it suit?

Listed sizes: 2000 × 1800 / 1500 / 900 mm. Plug type and voltage are configured for the destination country. Tell us your target market and we will confirm the exact size and specification.

Which mattresses can I use with it?

A powered base needs a mattress that flexes with the backrest and leg lift, such as foam, latex or a flexible hybrid. Send us the mattress type and thickness and we will confirm the fit before you order.

Which motor, control and function options are available?

Optional German OKIN or Haojiang motor. Optional wired remote, wireless remote or Sleepace app.

How is it packed and shipped?

Export packing and the loading plan are confirmed per order. Carton size, weight and container loading quantity for this model are available on request.
