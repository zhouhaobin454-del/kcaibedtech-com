Smart Beds FAQ

What is the standard MOQ?

Standard MOQ is from 20 pcs. Final MOQ varies by model and program requirements and is confirmed during quotation.

What is the typical lead time?

Typical lead time is around 45 days, varying by model, configuration and order quantity.

Can mattress construction be customized?

Mattress and comfort options vary by model. Include your target specification in your inquiry so the team can confirm availability.

Can Bluetooth or USB be added?

USB and Bluetooth options are available on selected smart bed configurations. Confirm with our team by model.

What upholstery options are available?

Knitted fabric and technology leather are available on selected models. Discuss your preferred look and program requirements with our team.

How do I choose between KR001–KR004?

Key differences are mattress model and construction (KD001-B1 latex / KD001-B2 breathable 3D / KD002-B1 graphene + split-head / KD003-B1 latex king), head-section (single vs. split) and bed size. KR002 also has the highest endurance rating at 15,000+ cycles. Use the comparison table above or contact us for guidance.
