KR002 Breathable Smart Adjustable Bed

The KR002 integrates a leather-upholstered bed frame with the KD001-B2 electric mattress (2000×1800×530 mm). The mattress combines 3D breathable material, Q-bounce cotton, high-density foam and independent pocket springs that reduce motion transfer. The lifting system is endurance-tested to over 15,000 cycles. Optional motors (OKIN / Haojiang), wireless / wired remote, voice control, APP control, vibration massage, LED lights and USB ports are available.

  • Backrest lift 0–55°, leg rest lift 0–35°
  • KD001-B2 mattress: 3D breathable material + Q-bounce cotton + pocket springs
  • Independent pocket springs reduce partner motion disturbance
  • Optional OKIN / Haojiang motor; 15,000+ cycle endurance tested
