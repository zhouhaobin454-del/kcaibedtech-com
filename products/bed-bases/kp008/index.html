KP008 Ottoman Gas Lift Bed Base Mechanism

The KP008 is a gas spring lift mechanism for ottoman-style storage bed bases. The gas strut assembly allows the bed base to lift smoothly for under-bed storage access. Available in single and double gas strut configurations. OEM/ODM customization available.

  • Gas spring strut lift mechanism
  • Smooth lifting action for ottoman storage access
  • Single or double strut configuration
  • OEM/ODM customization available
