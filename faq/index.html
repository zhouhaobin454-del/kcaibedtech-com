Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for product buyers and OEM / ODM partners.

Home›FAQ
What products does Kang Cai manufacture?

The current range includes adjustable bed frames (11 models), integrated smart beds (4 models), adjustable mattresses (3 models), electric leisure chairs (3 models) and bed bases (8 models). Visit the product collection to see all models.

Can you support OEM or ODM projects?

Yes. Kang Cai supports OEM and ODM programs. Share your product type, target market, expected order quantity and required functions so the team can confirm a suitable program.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

Standard MOQ is from 20 pcs. MOQ varies by product model and configuration. Contact our team with your specific requirements for confirmation.

What is the typical lead time?

Typical lead time is around 45 days. Lead time varies by product, order quantity and current production schedule. Final terms are confirmed during quotation.

Can product dimensions, fabrics or functions be customized?

Options depend on the model and production program. Include your preferred size, upholstery, motor, controls and target market in your inquiry so the team can confirm availability.

How can I request pricing or a sample?

Use the inquiry form and include the model, estimated quantity, delivery country and specifications. You can also reach us on WhatsApp.

What certificates or compliance documents are available?

Testing and market compliance documents vary by product and destination market. Contact Kang Cai to confirm which documents are currently available for your program.
