Frequently asked
questions.

Quick answers for product buyers and OEM / ODM partners. Contact our team if your project needs a specific configuration.

What products does Kang Cai manufacture?

The current range includes adjustable bed frames, integrated smart beds, adjustable mattresses and electric leisure chairs. Visit the product collection to see the models currently listed.

Can you support OEM or ODM projects?

Kang Cai’s official website describes OEM and ODM cooperation. Share the product type, target market, expected order quantity and any required functions so the team can confirm a suitable program.

Can product dimensions, fabrics or functions be customized?

Options depend on the model and production program. Include your preferred size, upholstery, motor, controls and target market in your inquiry so the team can confirm availability.

How can I request pricing or a sample?

Use the inquiry form and include the model, estimated quantity, delivery country and any required specifications. The inquiry form sends your details securely to Kang Cai for follow-up.

What certificates or compliance documents are available?

Selected product patent documents are shown on this website. Testing and market compliance documents vary by product and destination; contact Kang Cai to confirm current documents for your program.
