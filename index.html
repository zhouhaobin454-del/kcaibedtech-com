Questions buyers ask first

What is your minimum order quantity?

Standard MOQ is from 20 pcs. MOQ varies by model and configuration. Programs using German OKIN motors typically require higher minimums. Final MOQ is confirmed at the quotation stage.

What is the typical lead time?

Typical production lead time is around 45 days, subject to model, configuration and order quantity. Confirm your required delivery date during initial inquiry.

Which compliance documents are available?

Available test reports and compliance documentation vary by product and destination market. Contact Kang Cai to confirm which documents are available for your program and market.

Do you support private label?

OEM branding can be applied to product labels, the wireless remote, outer cartons and documentation. Specific options are confirmed by model and program.
